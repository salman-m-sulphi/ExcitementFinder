How the map is calculated

Speech volume and pace are compared with the same detected commentator’s usual delivery. The number of voices is estimated automatically. Unknown, overlapping and short-reference speech has no volume or pace evidence. Non-speech sound cues remain available. Speaker detection is imperfect; this is a review aid. No fixed 20-second spacing or top-twelve cap removes nearby candidates. The event threshold controls which peaks are shown.

Music, crowd noise, advertisements, and compression can cause false positives. Quiet action may be missed. Word boundaries differ across languages, and code-switching or overlapping speakers can reduce transcription accuracy. Compare moments within the same recording.
